How Halal Bridge works

No endless scrolling, no strangers messaging you out of nowhere. Just a careful, guided path from first profile to meeting in person — the way it should be.

01

Create your profile

Tell us about yourself, your deen and what you're looking for in a spouse. Only our team sees the full picture.

02

Get verified

We check your ID to confirm you are who you say you are. The document is deleted as soon as we've reviewed it.

03

We find your matches

Our team proposes matches based on what you're both looking for. There's no open directory for anyone to browse.

04

Both say yes first

You see a proposed match privately. Nothing moves forward unless you both ask to be introduced.

05

Talk, supervised

We open a conversation with our team in it throughout. Your wali or family can join too. No private numbers, no pressure.

06

Meet in person

When you're both ready, we arrange the introduction. Afterwards, each of you tells us privately how it went.

Questions

Good to know

What is the closed beta?

It's the first version of Halal Bridge, open only to people who registered here. Beta members get full access to matchmaking, and we'll ask for your feedback along the way so we can make it better before opening to everyone.

When does the beta open?

Once 1,000 people have registered. Everyone who registered gets an email with their access link as soon as it opens.

Who can join?

Single Muslims aged 18 and over who are sincerely looking for marriage. Every member's identity is checked before they can be matched.

Can people browse my profile?

No. There's no public directory. Your profile is only shown to someone our team has proposed as a match for you, and nothing moves forward unless you both agree.

Can my wali or family be involved?

Yes. Your wali can be added to your conversations, and our team is present in every one of them from the very first message.

What happens to my ID document?

It's used only to confirm your identity and date of birth, and it's deleted as soon as our team has reviewed it.

What do you do with my registration details?

We use them to send your beta access and to understand where our members are, so we can plan for every city. We never share or sell them.
